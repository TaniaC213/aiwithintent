1. The service

Out of Your Head is a done-for-you Claude AI setup service. It consists of four stages:

  • Discovery Call — 30 minutes, online, free. No commitment required.

  • The Reboot — 60 minutes, online. Your completed setup is handed over and walked through.

All sessions are conducted online. Session scheduling is managed through TidyCal.
