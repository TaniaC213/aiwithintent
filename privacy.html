Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Who we are

AI with Intent is operated by Tania Carcenac. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal information when you visit aiwithintent.com.au or engage with our services.

We are committed to handling your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

What information we collect

Website visitors. We use Google Analytics to collect anonymised data about how visitors use this site — pages visited, time on site, device type. This data is not personally identifiable and cannot be used to contact you.

Booking enquiries. When you book a call through TidyCal, TidyCal collects your name, email address and timezone on our behalf.

Email subscribers. If you sign up to receive emails from us, Flodesk collects your name and email address. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Clients. When you engage with Out of Your Head, we collect the following:

  • Name, email address and payment information — processed via Flodesk Checkout, powered by Stripe
  • Business information, voice, processes and knowledge shared during the Brain Dump session and throughout the service
  • Session notes taken by Tania during and after sessions
  • Audio or video recordings of sessions, if recorded via Fathom or Granola.ai (you will be informed if a session is recorded)

How we use your information

  • To deliver the Out of Your Head service to you
  • To communicate with you about your booking, build and delivery
  • To send you email updates — only with your explicit consent, and you can opt out at any time
  • To understand how visitors use the site and improve the experience (Google Analytics)

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information to train AI models.

How we store your information

Session notes and Brain Dump content are kept securely by Tania Carcenac and treated as strictly confidential. They are not shared with third parties outside of what is needed to deliver the service.

Recordings made via Fathom or Granola.ai are stored within those platforms, subject to their respective data retention policies.

Payment information is not stored by AI with Intent. It is processed and held securely by Stripe via Flodesk Checkout.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at tania@aiwithintent.com.au. We will respond within 30 days.

Complaints

If you believe we have handled your personal information in a way that does not comply with the Australian Privacy Principles, you are entitled to make a complaint. Please contact us first at tania@aiwithintent.com.au and we will do our best to resolve it. If you remain unsatisfied, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of this site after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
