Questions, answered

I'm really not a tech person. Will I keep up?

Yes. The first hour is designed to give non-technical people everything they need before they build anything. Every step runs in plain English. If you use Google Drive and send emails, you can do this day.

I've never used Claude before. Is this right for me?

Yes. Your prep will include getting Claude Pro, installing the desktop app and spending some time getting familiar with the basics before you arrive. The prep guide that comes with your booking walks you through exactly what to do.

Which Claude plan do I need?

Claude Pro or higher. Cowork runs on paid plans only. Pro is around $34 AUD a month and cancels anytime.

Does it work on Mac and Windows?

Both. Mac needs macOS 11 Big Sur or later. Windows needs Windows 10 or later.

My work laptop is managed by IT. Can I still come?

Bring a personal device — one you own and have full admin access to, where you can connect apps and adjust settings without IT approval.

Will what I build be specific to my business?

Yes. The structure is the same for everyone. What gets built — your voice, your context, your processes, your tools — is yours. The build prompt interviews you before it creates anything.

What about my client data going into AI?

We cover this properly in the morning session, not as a footnote. Short version: Claude doesn't train on your conversations on a paid plan, and your business brain files live on your own device. Safe, intentional use is built into the day.

What about ChatGPT's new Work tool — should I be looking at that?

ChatGPT recently launched something similar, which is genuinely good news — it means this whole approach is becoming standard practice, not just a Claude thing. My business runs on Claude and it's what I build for every client, mainly because it works directly with a folder on your device and produces plain, portable files rather than something locked inside one company's ecosystem. What you build on 19 August travels with you to whatever tools you use in the future.

Will you run this again?

Possibly. If August doesn't suit, email me at tania@aiwithintent.com.au and I'll let you know if another date comes together.
