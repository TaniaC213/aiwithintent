A system, not a single folder

Anthropic's own research names this as one of three things separating businesses that get real value from AI and those that don't: reliable data in one place.

That comes from setting up a system of folders organised around how your business actually works, department by department, so Claude always knows exactly where to look.

Your AI Brain/
├──About the Business.md← who you are, what you do, who it's for
├──Voice Profile.md← how you write, words you'd never use
├──skills/← skills that apply across the whole business
├──brand/← logo, colours, fonts, style guide
├──offers/← what you sell, your packages, your pricing
├──clients/
│   └──client-name/
│      ├──About This Client.md← context specific to this one client, outputs for them saved right here too
│      └──skills/← skills that only apply to this client
├──content/← emails, social posts, drafts, lead magnets, and anything Claude creates for this area
└──operations/← processes, templates, how you work

Skills don't all belong in one place either. A skill that applies across your whole business, your standard email format, your brand voice, sits in the top-level skills/ folder. A skill that only makes sense for one client or project, exactly how they like their reports formatted, lives inside that client's own folder instead, in its own skills/ subfolder. That way Claude only reaches for it when it's actually working on something for that client, not every time it writes an email for anyone.

The same goes for what Claude creates. Content it drafts for you lands in the content folder. Work it does for a specific client lands inside that client's own folder. You give Claude a starting structure and a short map of what's where, and it files what it makes into the relevant department, not one shared pile everything gets dumped into.

Most of what goes into these folders already exists somewhere, a brand guide, voice notes, process docs. The work here is gathering and organising it into one system Claude can actually use.

Claude isn't lost, it's looking where you told it to look

This is the single most common thing that goes wrong once someone's actually using their AI Brain day to day, and it's rarely talked about.

A Scheduled Task saved its output inside Claude's own default folder instead of the actual project folder, because nobody had told it exactly where to save to, so it looked finished but the file was nowhere anyone could find it.
Someone's whole folder lived inside Downloads, and it wouldn't link at all. Downloads is treated differently by most software for security reasons, so it's one of the few places your AI Brain shouldn't actually live.
Someone had two copies of the same folder without realising it, one synced from Google Drive, one being edited directly. Claude was reading from the synced copy, so nothing typed into the real one ever showed up.
A finished document landed in the very top level of someone's Google Drive instead of inside the actual client folder, because the destination given was "save it to Drive" instead of the exact folder.

Each of these takes under a minute to fix once you know to check it. Go take a look at where your own tasks and outputs are actually landing today.
