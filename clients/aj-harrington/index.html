What changes in practice

Two moments in your week

This isn't about what the setup does automatically. It's about what gets easier when you have a proper structure to work from.

✍️

Sitting down to write a newsletter

Now

Open ChatGPT. Try to explain your business from scratch. Get something that sounds nothing like you. Spend 45 minutes going back and forth. Give up and put it on next week's list. Or: think about doing it, close the tab, it's fine, you'll do it next month.

With your setup

Tell Claude what happened this month: a shoot you loved, something you noticed, what's coming up. A draft is structured to your template and sitting in MailerLite. You read it, change what doesn't feel right, and send.

It's not automatic. You still show up and do the work. But you're starting from something instead of nothing, in a format that already knows your voice.

💬

Briefing your VA for the month

Now

Your VA does a great job with what she has: your brand guide and access to your videos. The brief is essentially "do your thing." Content goes out, engagement is okay, growth is slow, and it's hard to know what to change because there's no strategy behind what's happening.

With your setup

A monthly check-in with Claude helps you think through this month's focus: what to push, what to hold off on, what pillar content matters most. Your VA gets a proper brief with actual direction.

How much traction you get depends on the strategy you build and how consistently you brief her. The skill gives you a framework. The results come from using it.
