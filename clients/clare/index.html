What changes in practice

Two moments in your week

This isn't about what the setup does automatically. It's about what gets easier when the repetitive steps aren't yours to carry anymore.

☕

Getting ready for Coffee at the Coast

Now

Ticket sold in Humanitix. Happy dance. Add a Trello card. Update the attendance tracker. Day before — write and send the reminder email. After the event — write and send the thank you email with everyone's handles. Add new attendees to MailerLite. Six steps. All Clare.

With your setup

Ticket sold. Claude picks up the Gmail notification, adds the attendee to the Sheet, and updates the attendance count. The day before, your reminder email draft is waiting for you to read and send. After the event, the thank you email is drafted with handles included. New attendees land in MailerLite.

You still read and approve everything before it goes out. But you're starting from a finished draft, not a blank screen — and the six manual steps are down to one: show up.

🧾

Keeping on top of receipts

Now

Receipts arrive in Gmail. You open the spreadsheet, track each one down, add it manually. You do it daily when you can, because you know if it piles up it becomes a bigger job. It's quick, but it's still you doing it every time.

With your setup

During your regular weekly admin block, Claude scans the receipts folder and adds anything new to your Google Sheet. You didn't have to think about it — it just happened while you were at your desk doing other things.

At the end of the month, you open the Sheet and the work is already done. You just check it looks right.
